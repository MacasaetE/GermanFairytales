Beauty in Fairytales

How fairytales teach us to see bodies and beauty

Learn More

Overview

Fairytales are one of the first forms of media children consume. These stories shape the way children learn to see the world, and the lesson they teach is that beauty is anything but just an adjective. Girls are complimented on their appearance and rewarded for patience, while being punished for curiosity. On the other hand, boys are praised for their strength and cleverness, and punished for showing emotion.

This website aims to demonstrate that Fairytales' emphasis on feminine beauty and masculine strength creates gendered expectations that shape how children learn to evaluate beauty at the cost of their self-worth and emotional expression.
