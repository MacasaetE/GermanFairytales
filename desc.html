Descriptions

Appearance

Character descriptions differ greatly from men to women in fairy tales.

Little Snow White by the Brothers Grimm:

"However, Little Snow White grew up, and when she was seven years old, she was so beautiful that her beauty surpassed even that of the queen, and when the queen asked her mirror:


“Mirror, mirror, on the wall,
who in this land is fairest of all?”

The mirror answered:

“You, my queen, may have a beauty quite rare,
but Little Snow White is a thousand times more fair.”" (171)

Little Snow White is described as so beautiful she surpasses the queen, inspiring jealousy from a grown woman.

Little Snow White by the Brothers Grimm:

"Now it happened that a prince came to the dwarfs’ cottage one day and wanted to spend the night there. When he entered the room and saw Little Snow White lying in the coffin... he couldn’t get enough of her beauty. Then he read the golden inscription and saw that she was a princess. So he asked the dwarfs to sell him the coffin with the dead Little Snow White inside. But they wouldn’t accept all the gold in the world for it. Then he pleaded with them to give Little Snow White to him as a gift, because he couldn’t live without gazing upon her..." (177)

Little Snow White's only character trait is her beauty, whereas the prince's looks are never described. He is only honorable and princely.
